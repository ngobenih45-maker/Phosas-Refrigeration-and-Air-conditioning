ABOUT PHOSAS

Keeping Your Home & Business Cool

Phosas Refrigeration & Air Conditioning is a commercial refrigeration and air conditioning company based in Kempton Park, Gauteng.

We provide installation, repair, servicing and maintenance of HVAC systems and refrigeration equipment for businesses, private homes, schools and commercial facilities.

Our highly trained and experienced technicians work with all major brands. We can supply and install the air conditioning brand you require and repair refrigeration equipment including shop display fridges, coldrooms and freezer rooms.
